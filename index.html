Help Center & FAQ

Frequently asked questions about U-Clean Bali services

How do I place a laundry order?

It is very easy! Simply click the WhatsApp button on this page, send your villa or hotel share-location pin, and our courier will come right away to pick it up.

Are my clothes washed with other people's clothes?

Not at all. We implement a strict **1 Machine Per Customer** system to guarantee the hygiene, cleanliness, and safety of your garments.

Is there any additional fee for pickup and delivery?

Free! Pickup and delivery services have no extra fees within our coverage areas (Sanur, Kuta, Seminyak, Canggu, Denpasar).

What if an item gets discolored or damaged?

We carefully sort colors before washing. Our professional team follows strict standard operating procedures to ensure your clothes are handled with utmost care.
